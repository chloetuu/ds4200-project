Analyzing Sector Performance and Top S&P 500 Stocks

This project looks at the performance of selected S&P 500 stocks and sector ETFs from 2022 to 2025. The goal is to compare relative growth, look at differences across sectors, and better understand how AI-related companies and sectors have affected the broader market.

Visualization 1: Growth of Selected Assets (Base 100 Comparison)

This visualization compares individual asset performance relative to the broader market, highlighting NVDA’s outsized growth compared to more stable assets like SPY.

Visualization 2: Sector ETF Monthly Return Heatmap

This heatmap shows monthly returns across sector ETFs, making it easier to identify patterns in strong and weak periods across different parts of the market.

Visualization 3: Top Stocks in the Selected Sector by Total Return

This bar chart compares the strongest-performing stocks within the selected sector and shows how a few companies can drive a large share of total sector performance.

Visualization 4: NVDA Candlestick Chart

This candlestick chart shows NVDA’s day-to-day price movement and gives a more detailed view of the volatility behind its overall upward trend.

Visualization 5: 30-Day Rolling Beta of NVDA vs SPY

This chart tracks NVDA’s rolling beta relative to SPY, showing how its sensitivity to overall market movement changed over time.

Interactive D3 Visualization: Sector Trends Explorer

This D3 visualization provides an interactive view of sector performance over time and helps show how technology began to separate from most other sectors after 2023.
